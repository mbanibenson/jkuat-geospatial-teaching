About the data

The facility register and every geostatistics dataset are synthetic. That is deliberate: a simulated field has a known variogram and a simulated register has known faults, so your work can be checked against the answer rather than against an opinion. The county boundaries are real, from geoBoundaries (public domain), because real polygons behave in ways synthetic ones do not.

The datasets are regenerated with a new random seed each year, so a set of answers from a previous cohort will not fit this year's data.

Working offline. Everything here totals about 9 MB, and the zips are self-contained. Download them once on a good connection and you can work through the whole unit without one. Ask if you would rather have it on a USB stick.

Course readers and synthetic datasets for JKUAT. The readers and generated data may be reused and adapted for teaching, with attribution. County boundaries are geoBoundaries ADM1 (public domain). Corrections and bug reports are welcome — an error you find in a reader is worth finding.
